### The Clinic

SoftCare stood in the same neighborhood under the name Şişli Med until 2013. The name changed; the nature of the work did not.
Clarity, record-keeping, and boundaries in a confidential file. Diagnosis is made in the exam room, not on GitHub.

<br>

The extension keeps a quick call/contact shortcut, ovulation and pregnancy-week calculations, symptom notes, and cycle tracking — in the browser. No data leaves the device; it stays in the browser's local storage (`storage.local`).

The Edge store listing is maintained separately.

<br>

### What lives in this org

What's kept open here: extension source, site utilities, translation files.
No patient records, consent forms, or procedure files live here.
